GREMLIN 13 BT - PRIVACY POLICY (PICTURESFRAMME APP)
Effective Date: October 8, 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION
The Picturesframme application developed by Gremlin 13 BT does NOT collect, harvest, or store any personal data, user credentials, or private information. All frame pricing data is fetched dynamically from the user's private Google Firebase Realtime Database.

2. CAMERA AND STORAGE PERMISSIONS
The application requires local device storage and camera access strictly for real-time picture framing calculation and displaying local frame asset textures. No data is transmitted to third-party servers.

3. DATA SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE
We fully comply with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement. We do not integrate any tracking SDKs, analytical tools, or targeted advertising networks. Your pricing data and image assets remain under your strict local ownership.

4. CONTACT INFORMATION
For legal and technical support, contact Gremlin 13 BT, Baja, Hungary.
